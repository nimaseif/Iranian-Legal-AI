Iranian Legal AI

About

Iranian Legal AI is an open-source project dedicated to collecting, organizing, and sharing legal resources related to Iranian law.

The project aims to support:

- Legal researchers
- Lawyers
- Law students
- Legal technology developers
- Artificial intelligence researchers

Areas

Criminal Law

Research materials related to criminal law and criminology.

Civil Law

Legal concepts, case studies, and educational materials.

Family Law

Legal guidance and educational resources.

Commercial Law

Business law and contract-related resources.

Contracts

Templates and legal drafting examples.

Goals

- Improve access to legal knowledge.
- Support legal AI research.
- Create structured legal datasets.
- Encourage collaboration among legal professionals.
